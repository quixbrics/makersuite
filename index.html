Maker Suite

Three free sound tools for student filmmakers. They run entirely in your browser: no accounts, no installs, nothing uploaded.

ScapeMaker

Soundscapes

A multi-track timeline for building a film's soundscape from your own recordings and freely licensed sounds, with licensing checked as you go.

Getting started: drop in your own recordings, or search the Internet Archive and the radio aporee map. A Freesound search is optional and needs your own free API key. Exports a single zip with the mix, stems and a sources list.

Open Source

ToneMaker

Emotional ambience

Generates a pad, a drone and an optional quiet melody, steered by where you place each one on a map of emotion. A clarity meter shows whether your tracks agree or clash.

Getting started: pick one of the preset scenes, then drag the tracks around the emotion map and listen to what changes. Save your own scenes, and export 24-bit WAV, with stems if you want them.

Open Source

FXMaker

Extreme effects

Stretch sounds up to 10,000x, re-pitch and reverse them, and push them through chains of 33 effects. A built-in safety limiter keeps the output from clipping.

Getting started: load up to four sounds and start with a recipe. Save your work as a .fxmaker project that carries its audio with it. MIDI control works in Chrome or Edge.

Open Source

Good to know

  • Use a desktop browser, preferably Chrome, and wear headphones for the best results.
  • Your settings and presets are stored in your browser only. Save project files to keep your work.
  • All three share the same look and a light/dark theme, which carries across the apps.
